# Gireve

> Gireve is a B2B digital platform for electric-vehicle charging and new-mobility services, connecting charge point operators (CPOs), e-mobility service providers (eMSPs), and drivers across Europe. Its interoperability hub powers EV roaming, clearing/transaction settlement, Plug & Charge, smart charging, and mobility data services, with 680,000+ connected charging points and ~1M monthly roaming transactions.

## About

- [Gireve homepage](https://www.gireve.com/): Company overview and solutions.
- [Solutions](https://www.gireve.com/en/solutions/): Digital platform (EV roaming, clearing, Plug & Charge, Advenir), consulting, and data services.
- [Connect Place (login)](https://connect-place.gireve.com/login/): B2B platform where connected operators manage roaming agreements and technical integration.

## Integration

- Gireve's roaming and clearing integration is delivered to connected CPOs/eMSPs through the OCPI-based interoperability hub; there is no public, self-serve developer portal or OpenAPI specification. Technical onboarding is handled through Connect Place after a signed agreement.
